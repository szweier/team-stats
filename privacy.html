Privacy Policy

Slow Pitch Book app and team stats website · Last updated October 25, 2026

The short version

Slow Pitch Book keeps your scorebook on your device. Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on a team website, and then only that team's game stats are uploaded. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Games, lineups, player names, plays, spray charts, seasons and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. One-player games are never uploaded.

What is uploaded when you create a team website

  • For each of that team's games: the date, team and opponent names, season or tournament name, score, line score, play-by-play, each player's name and stats, where balls were hit, the live game situation, and a picture of the scorebook.
  • A random team identifier, which is the team's website link, and a random scorer code.
  • An anonymous account identifier created by the app so only the team's scorers can change its page. It isn't linked to your name, email, Apple ID or device.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and is shown on the team's page to anyone who has the team's link. Don't add information you wouldn't want your team to see.

The website

The website reads the stats for the team link you open. It remembers which teams you've opened in your own browser's storage so you can switch between them; that list never leaves your browser. The website uses no cookies, analytics or ads. Your browser connects to Google Firebase to load stats and to GitHub Pages, which hosts the site; their standard server logs may include your IP address.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Team websites → your team → Delete Website & Its Data. This removes the team's page and every game on it from the server right away. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Children

Slow Pitch Book is a scorekeeping tool for adult recreational sports and isn't directed at children.
