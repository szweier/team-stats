Getting Started

Team Stats Beta · your team's schedule, availability, scorekeeping and stats in one place

1. Get it on your phone and sign in 2. Captains: set up your team 3. Schedule and who's coming 4. Keeping score 5. Making sure stats land on the right player 6. Reading the stats 7. Beta, problems and contact

1. Get it on your phone and sign in

Sign in (no password)

  1. Open the team's invite link from your captain (or the website), and enter your email.
  2. Open the sign-in email and tap the link. You're in. The same link creates your account the first time.
  3. Add your name: it's what teammates see on the schedule and in the stats.

No email? Give it a minute and check spam. If you asked twice, use the newest email: older links stop working.

Add it to your home screen (recommended)

  • iPhone: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).

The home screen app keeps its own sign-in. The first time you open it, enter your email there and tap the link in the email: it opens in Safari, which signs in, then switch back to the home screen app and it signs itself in within a few seconds.

2. Captains: set up your team

Start the team

During the beta, starting teams is invite-only. Once we've added you, sign in and tap Start a new team on your home screen, name it, and you're its admin. (Already keep the team in the Slow Pitch Book iPhone app? Use Have a team in the Stat Book app? with the team's scorer code instead, and its games come with it.)

Invite your teammates

Team tab → Invite teammates → Copy Link or Share…, then send it to the team (group text works well). Anyone with the link can join, so if it gets around, tap New Link and the old one stops working.

Give people roles

On the Team tab, each player has a role menu:

RoleCan do
AdminEverything: players and roles, invites, team colors, linking names, deleting games or the team.
ManagerThe schedule, lineups, keeping score and importing stats.
SchedulerAdd and edit games on the schedule.
ScorekeeperKeep score of a game once a manager has set its lineup.
PlayerAnswer "Can you make it?" and see the schedule and stats.

Every player can see the stats; roles only decide who can change things. Keep at least one other admin or manager in case you're away.

Team colors (admins)

Team tab → Team colors: pick a preset or your own main, highlight and background colors. The whole site takes them on for your team.

Bring in past stats from a CSV (optional)

Games → Keep Score → Import stats from a CSV (admins and managers). Pick the file, tick the seasons that belong to this team, and tap Import. Each game comes in finished and counts everywhere in the stats. Importing the same file again replaces those games instead of doubling them.

The file needs one row per player per game with these columns:

  • Team, Season (e.g. Fall 2026), Player, Game (vs. Hawks for home, at Hawks for away), Date (e.g. Tue, Sep 22, 2026, 2026-09-22 or 9/22/2026).
  • Batting: AB 1B 2B 3B HR RBI BB R H SF SO HBP. Pitching: GS IP ER W L. Missing or blank stats count as zero.

Our score is the runs our players scored; theirs is the runs our pitchers gave up, and the win or loss comes from the pitchers' W and L. Two games against the same team on the same day are split by the order of each player's rows. You can fix any imported score afterwards with Fix Opponent Score on the game.

3. Schedule and who's coming

  • Add games: Schedule → + Add Game (admins, managers and schedulers): date, time, opponent, field and notes. Drop a Pin on the Map gives everyone Apple Maps, Google Maps and Waze directions.
  • Can you make it? Everyone taps In, Maybe or Out on each game. The counts and Who's coming show the captain who'll be there.
  • Lineup: Set Lineup on a game (admins and managers) to pick the batting order from who's coming. That also lets a scorekeeper score it.

Home or away isn't set ahead of time: you pick who bats first after the coin toss, when you start keeping score.

4. Keeping score

  1. On the game day, tap Keep Score on the scheduled game (or Games → Keep Score → + New Game).
  2. Pick who bats first from the coin toss and the starting pitcher.
  3. Tap what happened on each play. The field shows the runners, outs and count, and you can change the pitcher at any inning break.
  4. Tap End Game when it's over.
  • No signal at the field? Keep going. Every tap is saved on the phone first and uploads when you're back online. Using the home screen app keeps those saved games safest.
  • Live for the team: while you score, teammates see the live score, inning, outs and who's up on the Games tab.
  • Two phones on one game: only one phone should score a game. If two do, the second is told and chooses which version to keep, so nothing is silently lost.
  • Mistakes: fix a finished game with Edit Game on its page; for app-scored or imported games use Fix Opponent Score.
  • Just tracking yourself? My Stats → Tracked Players → + Track One Player records one player's at-bats (yours or your kid's on another team). Only you see them.

5. Making sure stats land on the right player

Stats are kept under the name the scorer typed. To make them count as a teammate's (so they show in that person's My Stats and follow them if they change their name), an admin links the two:

  • Link: Team tab → the player → Link, then pick the name(s) they were scored under. Link More adds another name; Unlink undoes links made here.
  • Same name: a scorebook name that exactly matches someone's account name counts as them automatically.
  • Typos and duplicates: Team tab → Scorebook names → Rename fixes a misspelled name or merges two names into one person. Undo brings it back.

6. Reading the stats

  • Season: tap the record at the top right to pick a season, a tournament, or All seasons. It applies to every page.
  • Stats → Leaders: the top five in each category. Best Offensive Player is a rating where 50 is the team's average hitter; on-base and slugging count most, then RBI and runs per game. Averages only count regulars (2 plate appearances a game).
  • Stats → All Players: the full table. Tap a column to sort.
  • Stats → Over Time: pick any player to see their running AVG, OBP, SLG and OPS game by game. Tap the chips to turn lines on and off, and tap the chart for the numbers at any game.
  • My Stats: your own numbers, chart, game log and spray chart. The menu at the top switches between this team, All my teams, or any other team you're on.
  • Spray: where balls were hit, for the team or one player.

7. Beta, problems and contact

Team Stats is in beta, so you may hit the odd bug. When you do, tap Report a problem at the bottom of any page and tell us what happened. It sends the page you were on and any recent errors along with it, which helps us fix it fast. Errors are also logged automatically.
